Creation of individual coffee and tea sets

Taste, mood and attention to detail in every gift

There are gifts that are simply pleasant to receive. And there are those that you want to examine, open slowly, taste, share with loved ones, and remember the person who gave them.

This is exactly how we create individual coffee and tea sets in our cafe.

We believe that good coffee and aromatic tea are much more than just beverages. They represent a cozy morning, a few minutes of silence in the middle of a busy day, a long conversation with a loved one, a favorite cup in your hands, and a small daily ritual that makes life more enjoyable.

That's why we assemble each of our sets with more than just beautiful ingredients. We strive to create a unique combination of flavors, aromas, and details that suits a specific person, occasion, or mood.

☕ A set created just for you

Everyone has their own preferences. Some start their day with a strong, rich coffee, others prefer soft, creamy notes, and still others especially enjoy the fruity and floral notes of a good tea.

That is why we offer custom-made coffee and tea sets .

You can tell us who you're choosing a gift for, the occasion, the recipient's favorite flavors, and how you envision the set itself. We'll help you find a harmonious combination.

The set may include:

  • selected coffee with different flavors and aromas;
  • classic and unusual tea varieties;
  • herbal and fruit compositions;
  • sweets and small additions to drinks;
  • accessories for cooking and beautiful serving;
  • gift wrapping;
  • a postcard with your wishes;
  • additional elements that will make the gift even more personal.

The result is not a standard gift from the shelf, but a set with its own character and mood .

🍵 For those who love tea

A tea set can become a real journey of tastes.

For classic tea lovers, we'll help you choose fine black or green tea varieties. For those who enjoy experimenting, we offer intriguing blends with berries, fruits, herbs, flowers, and spices.

You can put together a set for quiet evenings at home, cheerful mornings, cozy winter gatherings, or leisurely weekends.

Imagine: several carefully selected varieties of tea, beautiful packaging, your favorite sweets, and a small card with warm words. Such a gift seems to say, "I know what you like. I chose this especially for you."

It is attention to a person that makes even a small gift truly valuable.

☕ For true coffee connoisseurs

Coffee is a whole world of aromas and flavors.

Nutty, chocolate, caramel, fruity, and berry flavors—one coffee variety can be noticeably different from another. We'll help you create a coffee set for both those just starting to appreciate good coffee and those who are a true connoisseur.

You can choose several options and turn the gift into a small coffee tasting .

This set is especially pleasant to open in the morning. Today it's one flavor, tomorrow another, and after a few days, your favorite will emerge.

If you're unsure what kind of coffee someone prefers, just tell us a little about them. We'll suggest versatile and interesting pairings that will help you explore the different nuances of coffee and enjoy every brew.

🎁 A gift for any occasion

The good thing about a personalized coffee or tea set is that there is almost always a suitable occasion for it.

A birthday, anniversary, holiday, thank you gift, a get-together with friends, a housewarming, a small token of appreciation, or simply a desire to please someone —the set can be tailored to suit a specific occasion.

And sometimes no reason is needed at all.

A small box of good tea or coffee, accompanied by sweets and a warm note, can be a wonderful way to say:

"Thank you".

"I'm thinking about you."

"Let today be a little nicer."

"This is just for you - to put you in a good mood."

And that's the special charm of such gifts: they are suitable for both important events and small everyday expressions of care.

✨ Corporate coffee and tea sets

We can also create custom packages for companies, employees, clients and business partners .

Corporate gifts work especially well when they're not overly formal. Good coffee, aromatic tea, and beautiful accessories create a sense of warmth and attention while maintaining the elegant and universal nature of the gift.

These sets can be prepared for holidays, professional events, meetings with partners, corporate events, or used as a thank you gift to employees and clients.

We'll help you choose the right contents based on the occasion, number of sets, preferences, and budget, so the entire series looks harmonious and dignified.

🤎 We're not putting together a box, we're creating a mood.

For us, it's not just what's inside the set that matters, but also the impression it makes.

That's why we pay attention to every detail: the combination of flavors, the filling, the presentation, and the overall atmosphere of the gift.

The set can be simple and elegant, cozy and homey, festive and vibrant, or discreet and businesslike. It all depends on who you're giving it to and for what occasion.

You can choose a small token of appreciation or create a more extensive gift set with several types of coffee and tea, sweets, and additional accessories.

There's no need to navigate the myriad varieties and combinations on your own. Tell us your preferences, and we'll help you create a design that will be a joy to give and receive.

🌿 Little rituals that make your day better

There is something wonderfully warm about a cup of your favorite drink.

The smell of fresh coffee in the morning. A kettle gently boiling in the evening. Steam over a cup. A few leisurely minutes. Conversation at the table. A book, a blanket, and fragrant tea on a rainy day.

When we give good coffee or tea, we are in a sense giving a person these little moments of pleasure .

That's why a personalized set is more than just a beautiful gift.

It's an invitation to stop for a few minutes, make your favorite drink, and enjoy the moment.
